# After-action review: zone-death fix

Moderator, iteration 2. Run: 100 matches, seeds 1000–1099, one M1 bot against 15 copies of `example_bot.py`, same as AAR 1. Raw output: `notes/tests/2026-11-21-m1-vs-example.json` (overwritten by this run; the AAR 1 version is in git at bac94a0).

## Change tested

- `survival.hold_point`: when the mech is already deep enough inside the next circle, stay where it is; otherwise compute the hold point once per next circle and keep it (it used to be recomputed from the current position every tick, so Nav's goal moved as the mech moved).
- `brain._zone_go`: while inside the live circle and within `radius + 12` m of its edge, a Nav move that points outward is replaced by a move straight toward the center (Nav's detours around buildings could step out of the circle).
- Tests added for both (`test_hold_point_stays_stable_while_mech_moves`, `test_zone_path_cannot_steer_outside_live_circle`); 65 tests pass.

Six of the seven zone deaths are gone, so they had one shared cause after all: a hold point that moved with the mech, and detours stepping out of the circle.

## What went wrong

- **One zone death left:** seed 1042, died at 199.05 s with 3 HP, after 150 ticks (7.5 s) outside the circle; it had taken 1035 damage, none from behind. Cause not yet known: possibly a fight or a cover move that pushed it out, then the zone finished it.
- **Late answers 0.005%** (was 0) and a max answer time of 36.7 ms in seed 1042. Inside the 2% check limit, but over the 35 ms p99 guardrail's spirit for single ticks; machine load from other work may explain it.
- **Win rate still 4%** (examples 6.4%) and kills 0.36 vs 0.94. M1 survives to mid places but doesn't close out matches. No match reached 415 s, so the final-collapse plan is still untested.
- The +0.15 score gain is inside the noise for 100 matches (about ±0.5); the zone-death drop is the real signal.

## Expected vs observed

Expected: zone deaths to 0% with little effect elsewhere. Observed: 1% and a small score gain. As expected apart from the one remaining death.
