# Review: squad sim (`sim/bots/squad.py`, `sim/inproc.py`), 2026-11-22

Critic. Scope: does the squad model let us tell good strategies from bad ones? No code changed.

## Evidence

Tiny run: `python sim/inproc.py --bot s=sim/versions/s_base/bot.py --count 1 --bot squad=sim/bots/squad.py --count 15 --matches 2 --jobs 2 --seed 7`
```
squad: n=30 score=7.9 causes={'gun': 2, 'zone': 19, 'npc': 7} kills=0.07 death_t_median=394.85
    s: n=2  score=1.0 causes={'gun': 2} kills=1.0 death_t_median=148.25
```
28 of 30 squad bots died, 19 to the zone, 7 to NPCs, 2 to us. Median squad death 395 s. We died at ~148 s.

## Findings, ranked by impact on telling strategies apart

1. **The score is a floor effect, not a broken sim** (`sim/engine.py:65 placements`, `inproc.py:213`)
   - What: place = mechs alive just before our death. Squad bots never kill each other, and in the sim they die almost only late (zone burn, ~395 s). So score = (our kills) + (squad zone/NPC deaths before our death).
   - Problem: every strategy that dies before ~350 s scores 0–2, whether it dies at 60 s or 300 s. Survival time, the thing strategies change, is invisible in the score. That is why "nothing moves it". The track (1.6) has the same shape, so this is likely real, not a sim bug.
   - Fix: make `death_t` (mean, and % alive at 300/400 s) and kills the primary proxies; keep score as the target. Run strategies on paired seeds and compare death_t. A change that moves median death from 150 s to 250 s is real progress even at score 1.9.
   - If skipped: we keep throwing away good changes because the score cannot see them.

2. **Squad is never in danger until the final burn** (`squad.py:143-144`)
   - What: squad bots only head to `next_center` once already outside (`in_zone` false). 19/30 die to zone.
   - Problem: the late-game reward (outlasting bots that burn) is huge in the sim (+10 places if we just survive to ~400 s) and unknown on the track. If track bots handle the zone better, the sim overstates hiding/surviving strategies.
   - Fix: none needed for ranking strategies now (we die long before). Before trusting any "survive to the end" strategy, check one recorded track replay or the track page for house bots dying in zone. Until then treat sim win% as optimistic.

3. **Shared vision plus 60 m defend range = near-constant contact** (`squad.py:105-127`, `DEFEND_RANGE`)
   - What: any sighting is shared for 30 s; hunters walk to it with velocity extrapolation; all 15 bots crowd the next circle and every one fires within 60 m.
   - Problem: the track says "one early, more as you thin them out". Here non-hunters also engage, so in a shrinking circle we fight many bots, not one. This is the likely too-harsh part: it compresses early death time for every strategy alike.
   - Fix: non-hunters fire only if fired upon (a `damaged` event by us) or under 25 m; shared positions get a 1.0 s delay (squad radio latency, a guess). Then calibrate (below).

4. **Aim error is small for upper tiers** (`squad.py:176`)
   - What: noise is an AR(1) filter: stationary error = 0.2·2.2·noise/√(1−0.64) ≈ 0.73·noise. Easy ≈ 0.044 rad (4.4 m at 100 m), expert ≈ 0.006 rad (0.6 m at 100 m, near-perfect), with full lead.
   - Problem: half the squad (hard + expert) hits a 4.5 m-tall mech at any range in its gun envelope. Dodging/strafing/peeking strategies cannot gain against near-perfect aim, so they all look the same.
   - Fix: per-tier aim noise ×2 for hard/expert (0.03, 0.016), and add tracking lag: aim follows the solution with a per-tier turn limit, so lateral movement costs them hits.

5. **Reaction delay applies only on first sight by that bot** (`squad.py:109-111,179`)
   - What: `first_seen` is per bot and cleared when we leave its view. A hunter guided by shared vision still waits `react` once it sees us. OK. Minor: crates/NPCs skip reaction (fine).
   - No fix needed. Listed so nobody "fixes" it.

6. **Checked, no bug found**
   - `summarize` score = mean(n − place), n = 16 slots; correct for 16 − place.
   - Squad deaths are counted: `_hunters` treats a member silent for >1 s as dead, so hunter count does grow.
   - `_REG` reset: a new match re-adds slot 0, which triggers a reset; per-process, so `--jobs` is safe.
   - Tiers by join order = slot order among squad slots; `--rotate` moves our slot, tiers stay 4/4/4/3. Fine.
   - Loot: medium+ take lasers, shoot crates and NPCs they are hit by; upgrades by PREF. Easy does not loot. Plausible.
   - Zone damage applies to squad (19 zone deaths), so they do suffer the burn.

## Calibration

Targets: current track bot (f803b02) 1.6, example bot 1.15. Sim today: current ~1.9–2.0 (too easy by ~0.3, not too harsh). Compare example bot first: if the sim gives it ~1.4, the offset is uniform and ranking is still valid.

Plan (smallest first):
1. Run example + f803b02, 56 seeds each, paired. Record score and death_t.
2. If both are high by a similar amount, accept the offset; rank by death_t.
3. If the gap between them is wrong (sim gap ≠ 0.45), apply fix 3 then 4, one at a time, re-run step 1, stop at the first setting within ±0.2 of both targets.

## Verdict

No scoring bug. The score cannot see survival time because the squad only dies late. Rank strategies by death_t and kills on paired seeds; then calibrate with fixes 3 and 4.
