# After-action review: M1 vs example x15

Tester, iteration 1. Run: 100 matches, seeds 1000-1099, one M1 bot and 15 copies of `example_bot.py`. Full per-match output: `notes/tests/2026-11-21-m1-vs-example.json`.

## What went right

- M1 averaged 8.58 points (mean place 7.42), against 7.42 points (mean place 8.58) for the 1500 example-bot mech-matches in those same games.
- Time outside the zone was 0.35% of alive ticks, below the 2% guardrail. Rear-hit damage fell to 9.8% from the example bots' 48.5%.
- Gun hit rate at 50-150 m was 85.5% (910 counted shots), above the 40% proxy. HP with at most three mechs alive averaged 80.0% of max (18 samples).
- The bot had no exceptions, malformed lines, or late answers in the batch. The recorded-stream check passed directly and in Docker with the arena mounted.

## What went wrong

- Seven M1 deaths (7%) were caused by the zone, versus 3.1% for the example bots. This misses the 0% target. The bot spent little time outside overall, so a few severe displacements or difficult late transitions account for the deaths.

- The M1 win rate was 4%, below the examples' 6.4%, and kills averaged 0.32 versus 0.94. M1 does not farm cubes or weapons in this simulator, and it declines many fights; the better midrange accuracy does not translate to more kills.
- The 85.5% gun hit rate comes from only 910 shots while examples made 35,427. The target selection and fight rules bias this metric toward favorable, low-volume shots; it does not establish general aim quality.
- The simulator is still phase 1: no laser, mortar, lightning, weapon pickups, crates, cubes, upgrades, NPCs, drops, or tree visibility. It cannot validate economy or weapon-matchup decisions.

## Expected vs observed

The plan expected M1 to improve placement through zone discipline, selective fighting, cover, and outward facing. Placement improved by 1.16 points against the example field; outside-zone time and rear-hit share improved substantially. However, zone deaths persisted, and fewer kills plus a 4% win rate show that M1 is not yet a robust endgame bot. The phase 1 results are directional because combat and loot are simplified.

During the code review, the Critic's N1 plan fix was found missing from `brain.py`: an empty cover search still meant fight at any distance. The code now treats the bot as cornered only when the attacker is within 60 m or energy is below 20; otherwise it boosts away and faces the attacker. A 30 ms guard now skips cover search on an over-budget tick and reuses a recent point only when the threat positions remain within 10 m. The 100-match batch was rerun after this fix.

## Replan

1. Keep M1's facing, selective aim, and zone-first priority; they improve rear damage and outside-zone time.
2. Inspect the seven zone deaths by seed, position, and phase. Fix navigation or deadline transitions before adding more combat behavior.
3. Complete simulator phase 2, starting with weapons and pickups, then cubes/upgrades, NPCs, and drops. The current M1 score comparison cannot assess those systems.
4. Add multiple opponent styles after phase 2; the example-only pool is not enough to choose a robust strategy.
