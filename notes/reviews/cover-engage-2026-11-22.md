## Required guardrails before any implementation or A/B

1. **One-state integration:** no standalone persistent camp mode; reuse `_fight`/`_evade` and normal cover selection.
2. **Exposure:** ≤3 s at the firing/peek position per attempt; at most one reuse-free success or failure at a spot.
3. **Threat gate:** isolated real target, no second real visible/remembered threat inside 200 m, no enemy likely to arrive from recent shared vision/sound.
4. **Geometry:** verified LOS break, map-walkable, hidden at both target-height checks, inside next circle, 4 m clearance, and reachable by 3 s.
5. **Zone:** setup only when remaining shrink time covers route ETA + setup + 5 s margin; cancel into `zone` on urgency.
6. **Combat:** `_ttk_ratio`, `_may_fire`, FIRE_POLICY, reload/laser-charge safety, rear damage, and no target during another hunter's damage window.
7. **Economy:** never displace zone/regen/safe drop/cube/loot; max one off-route detour.
8. **Exit:** after first shot or failure, break LOS and move at least 60 m to a different zone-safe point; do not stand/recharge at the firing point.
9. **Failure gates for A/B:** reject the tactic if rear-hit share rises, exposure seconds rise, early deaths rise, outside-zone ticks rise, or deaths within 20 s of ambush firing rise.
