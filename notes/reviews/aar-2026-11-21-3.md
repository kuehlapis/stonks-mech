# After-action review: first track submission

Moderator, iteration 2. Submission: commit `f803b02` (iteration 2, aggressive style) at https://github.com/kuehlapis/stonks-mech. Track result: **1.6** mean over the practice matches (48 matches, about ±0.7 noise per the objective).

## What went right

- The build was accepted: it built, passed the scorer's conformance check and played its matches. M0 is confirmed on the real track.
- The squad simulator predicted 1.71–1.74; the track gave 1.6. That's inside the noise, so **the squad sim is calibrated**: two data points now agree (example bot 1.01 sim vs 1.15 track; our bot 1.71–1.74 vs 1.6). Local results can be trusted for A/B decisions.
- 1.6 beats the example bot's 1.15 on the track.

## What went wrong

- **1.6 is far below the 6.00 best and the 12.00 objective.** Root cause: the format is us against a 15-bot squad that hunts us, and points come from kills (15 − squad bots alive when we die). At about 0.8 kills per match (sim), we score about 1.7. Reaching 6 needs about 5 kills per match on average.
- Our first plan (survive, avoid fights) was built for a free-for-all that doesn't exist on the practice track. The fix happened only after the teammate read the track page. Lesson: read the scoring environment first.
- Duels: we win about 45% against hard and expert bots (sim), and lasers cause many of our deaths.
- Known open risk shipped: the hold-point search can take 50–62 ms in the worst case (test_survival.TestTiming fails), over the 50 ms budget. Not yet known whether it cost points.

## Replan (next iteration, in order of expected gain)

1. **Win more duels against hard and expert bots** (45% now): lasers are the main killer, so dodge laser lines (break line of sight when `charging` is seen), and take or keep a laser ourselves. Measure: duel win rate vs each tier in the squad sim.
2. **Kill faster, one hunter at a time:** the squad sends hunters weakest-first and in growing numbers, so fight each one early, near cover, before the next arrives; retreat to regen between kills. Measure: kills per match (0.8 now; target 3+ next).
3. **Fix the 62 ms hold-point search** (cap or spread across ticks) so no tick is dropped. Measure: TestTiming passes, check.py max < 45 ms.
4. Resubmit whenever the squad sim improves by more than 0.3 (2 × its SE): up to 4 submissions per hour are allowed before Sunday 11:00 AM EST.
