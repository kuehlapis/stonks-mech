Critic, 2026-11-22. Scope: `git diff python/` (brain.py `STRAFE_BOOST_E`/`STRAFE_JUMP`, survival.py `HOLD_SEARCH`) and `notes/ideas/movesets.md`. Rules from `protocol.md`.

## Findings, ranked by impact

| # | Sev | Finding | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | high | **Hop makes the dodge worse, not better.** In the air acceleration is `air_accel` 12 m/s² vs 58.5 on the ground. A hop lasts ~1.0 s, so during it we cannot reverse the strafe (reversing 11.7 m/s takes ~2 s in the air); the horizontal path becomes a straight, predictable line for 1 s, and every strafe reversal (0.25–0.7 s) falling inside the hop is lost. The vertical arc is only 2.75 m on a ~9 m tall cylinder, so a straight-line lead still hits the body. The proposal's "gravity curve breaks the lead" misreads the movement model. | protocol.md L279 (`ground_accel` 58.5, `air_accel` 12); movesets.md "Jump speed" row (1.0 s, 2.75 m); L284 head clearance 9 m | Keep `STRAFE_JUMP = 0`. Run `m_hop` only as a confirming A/B; do not ship it on noise. If kept, hop only when no shooter has LOS (it is useless as a dodge). |
| 2 | high | **Boost + jump in the same action = jet climb.** When airborne, `boost` jets upward (15 energy/s, up to 10 m/s rise) instead of running. In `m_both`, every hop with energy > threshold turns into a jet: we rise slowly and predictably (easiest possible target), burn energy, and float above cover. The flee path (`boost=True`) has the same issue whenever a hop fires. | protocol.md L227 ("In the air: jet upward ... draining `jet_drain`/s") | In `_strafe_move`: `boost = boost and me.get("grounded", False)` (one line, also stops accidental jets after knockback). Discard `m_both` results gathered without this fix. |
| 3 | medium | **Boost-strafe starves lightning and escape energy.** Boost drains 17/s and regen only starts after 0.6 s idle (L287), so with `STRAFE_BOOST_E = 40` energy pins at ~40 during any fight and never refills while we keep spending. Lightning costs 3/shot at 4 shots/s = 12/s (L306) and combat gates on `energy >= cost + 2` (combat.py L102): boost (17/s) + lightning (12/s) drains 29/s, so lightning holders go dry in seconds and `CORNER_ENERGY` 20 / flee `e > 15` lose their reserve. Boost is also louder (L397), revealing our bearing to the squad. | protocol.md L227, L287, L306, L397; brain.py `CORNER_ENERGY`; combat.py L102 | Boost-strafe only when not holding lightning and energy > 60 (leave ≥ 40 for a flee/jet). A/B `m_boost` must report mean energy at death and lightning shots fired, not only placement. |
| 4 | low | **Hop trigger `self._strafe_t == t`** works (same float assigned this tick), but also fires on wall/zone-blocked flips inside the loop (L569), i.e. hops next to walls and the zone edge, where a 1 s uncontrollable drift (finding 1) can carry us into the wall or outside the zone. `rng.random()` is only drawn when the other conditions pass, so `STRAFE_JUMP` changes the RNG stream of later strafe timings: A/B variants are not same-seed comparable for strafe behaviour. | brain.py L546–548, L569, L579 | Use a flag set only in the timed reversal branch; draw `rng.random()` unconditionally or from a separate `random.Random`. |

But ~40 lines of dead search code remain and `HOLD_DEPTH`/`HOLD_CANDS`/`HOLD_NODES` constants now do nothing. | survival.py diff L72 | If the A/B confirms no loss, delete the search block and its constants rather than keep a flag (smallest code). |

## Proposals in movesets.md

| Move | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Boost-strafe | Test, with finding 3 fix | Real gain vs gun leads at range (67% more lateral travel), but energy cost is the hidden price. Against a 15-bot squad, being heard and energy-dry may cost more than it saves. Expect small effect; decide on ≥ 2 SE. |
| Hop | Reject (finding 1, 2) | Misreads air control. |
| Backpedal facing enemy | Keep | Rear ×1.5 is real. |
| Duck within 30 m / peek after beam | Most promising | Laser is hitscan (only LOS blocks it); 1.65 s cooldown window is a rule fact. Against a squad, though, peeking one laser exposes us to the others: peek only when ≤ 1 laser holder has LOS. |
| Jet to roof as escape | Low priority | `HOLD_ROOF` already tested no gain; jet is slow and visible. |
| Silent walk | Cheap, plausible | Sound rule L397 is exact; fits "hide and outlast" vs a hunting squad, which is where placement comes from. |
| Matchup table | Mostly correct | Check: "never pick up a mortar" is fine, but mortar beats lightning (L311) and lobs over walls — the one weapon that hits a hider. Worth an A/B, not a rule. |

## Verdict
Ship nothing from the brain.py diff as on: keep both knobs at off, add the `grounded` guard to boost (finding 2) regardless. `HOLD_SEARCH = False` is fine to merge. Put effort into peek-after-beam and silent walk over hop/boost.
