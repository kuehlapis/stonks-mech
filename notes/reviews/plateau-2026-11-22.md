# Review: `notes/ideas/plateau.md` (P1–P4), 2026-11-22

Critic. Checked against `protocol.md`, `hello.config` (from `check/stream.jsonl.gz`), `sim/bots/squad.py`, `python/survival.py` (ZONE_LEAN), and `python/brain.py:54` (RUN_MODE). No sims run.

## Key numbers

- Boost: `boost_drain` 17/s, `energy_regen` 18/s only after 0.6 s of not spending, 100 energy. So one full burst is about 5.9 s (≈115 m at 19.5 m/s), then about 6.2 s of recovery at walk speed. Pulsed average ≈ 15.5 m/s vs a hunter's 11.7 m/s: we gain about 4 m/s. Keeping a 40-energy reserve roughly halves the bursts.
- Gun range 230 m, laser 280 m (`protocol.md:303,305`). The hunter does not need to close; it shoots from where it is.
- Zone end times (cumulative): 110, 195, 265, 325, 375 s, and radius 0 at 415 s.
- Squad memory: `squad.py:118` keeps a shared sighting **30 s**, not 5 s. Hunters walk to it, extrapolated by velocity up to 3 s. Once nothing is seen for 30 s, hunters fall back to roaming. Any squad member's sighting refreshes it.
- Squad bots ignore sounds completely (no sound handling in `squad.py`). They boost or jet only when outside the zone or stuck (`squad.py:211-213`).

## Findings, ranked

1. **P1 works by breaking line of sight, not by outrunning. Fix fact 4.** The extra ~4 m/s from boost pulses takes about 30 s to open 130 m, which is how far we must get to leave gun range when a hunter is 100 m away. That whole time we are under near-perfect hard and expert aim (squad review, finding 4). So "a hunter at 100 m never closes" is true but irrelevant.
   - **Fix:** in RUN_MODE, the goal is the nearest LOS break (building, or dense canopy, see 2). The far point of the next circle comes only after LOS is broken. Spend boost only to reach LOS-break cover.
   - Then stay hidden ≥ 30 s. That is the sim's actual memory window, and the trigger for regen (5 s) plus losing the hunt (30 s).
2. **Rule misread: "never trees".** Trees do not stop shots, but crowns and trunks **do block vision** past the 45 m near range (`protocol.md:369-372,452`). Shared vision is the threat, so a dense grove hides us from sight, and the sim's squad never fires at sounds.
   - **Fix:** allow trees as a hide when no hunter is within 45 m. Prefer buildings when a hunter is close, because bots on the track may fire blind into trees at a sound (`:317`).
3. **The sim over-rewards P1 and P3 in three ways (tell the moderator).**
   - (a) Squad bots never use sound, but our boost is heard at 90 m (`protocol.md:393`), so on the track running on boost gives away our bearing.
   - (b) Squad bots never boost or jet to chase us.
   - (c) Non-hunters never leave the next circle.
   - A track bot with any one of these closes the gap P1 and P3 rely on. **Mitigation:** in the A/B, also run a squad variant where hunters boost and use sound bearings (a ~10-line change to `squad.py`, Tester-owned). Keep a P1 gain only if it survives both versions.
4. **P3 vs ZONE_LEAN: same function, opposite targets.** ZONE_LEAN (`survival.py:120`) moves the hold point inside the *next* circle, away from the visible mechs. P3 replaces the hold point with the *current* edge band, away from `next_center`.
   - **Conflict:** with both on, one silently overrides the other depending on code order.
   - **Fix:** when RUN_MODE+P3 is on, skip ZONE_LEAN until the final two phases. Then P3 hands over and ZONE_LEAN applies. Make one the explicit `if/elif` of the other, and test the order.
   - **More P3 problems:**
     - The far-side edge moves inward at up to (Δr + Δcenter)/shrink_s ≈ 7.2 m/s (phase 1: 180+180 over 50 s). That is above the 5 m/s silent-step limit (`protocol.md:397`), so P3 and P1's "walk silently" cannot both hold while the zone shrinks.
     - Any zone damage resets regen (`:291`), so the `radius − 12` margin must hold while moving.
     - The start circle (r 560) goes past the map edge. Clamp the band to walkable in-bounds points.
5. **P2 is right, with two checks.**
   - (a) Plating is +15% max HP ×3 (`hello.config`, upgrades). It only helps if we then regen up to the new max, so "break contact 5 s at ~395 s" must come before the 160/260 HP/s phases (from 375 s), not at 395 s. During the burn every second outside costs 260 HP and no regen runs (`:291`, `:418`).
   - (b) The 210 s drop has a 600 m landing sound and a 4 s capture (`drops`, `:395`). Every bot on the track hears it, so the "no squad within 80 m" gate is necessary but not enough. Also require no hunter seen in the last 10 s.
   - **Sim over-reward:** squad bots die to the zone in the final phases (finding 2 of the squad review). The stated payoff of "~5–6 sim" is upside the track may not have. The plan already says so.
6. **P4 is fine but small; keep it last.** In the sim the weakest hunter is easy-tier (`squad.py:68` rank, `_hunters` sorts ascending). Each kill adds a hunter (`n = 1 + dead`), so after one kill there are 2 hunters, and after 150 s another one is added anyway. That cost is in the plan. "Alone" must mean no squad bot seen within 150 m **and** no other shared sighting in 5 s. A non-hunter inside 60 m joins the fight (`DEFEND_RANGE`).

## Verdict

Build P1 with fix 1 (LOS first, then distance) and fix 2 (trees allowed past 45 m). Stack P2 with fix 5a (regen before 375 s). Gate P3 against ZONE_LEAN (fix 4). P4 last. Judge on death_t and % alive at 300/400 s, on both the current squad and a squad that boosts and hears (fix 3). Otherwise the sim will crown P1/P3 for reasons the track does not share.
