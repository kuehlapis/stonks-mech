## Still not simulated

- `max_slope_deg` (50°) walking limit, overhang slide-out and roof-edge slide-off (phase 1 shortcuts, unchanged).
- Debug drawing caps (bots' `debug` is ignored; it never affects a match).
- NPC pathing: drones walk straight and stop at walls (no navigation).

## Assumptions (marked `# assumption:` in the code)

Weapons
1. Laser damage is linear in charge: `damage × (min_frac + (1 − min_frac) × charge/charge_s)`; charge accrues from the first tick.
2. Lightning cone: target bearing within `cone_deg` of the aim yaw on either side (60° total); range is 3D muzzle-to-center; no shot (and no energy spent) below the energy cost.
3. Mortar direct hit deals `damage` only (not damage + splash); the direct target is fully concussed. Splash distance is measured to the target's cylinder surface. A shell explodes where its `ttl` runs out. A shell never direct-hits its own shooter.
4. `knock_up` adds to any upward speed; concussion makes the mech airborne.
5. NPCs and crates take no rear multiplier or armor; damaging them resets your regen timer.

Loot, cubes, upgrades
6. Floor loot, crates and NPC homes are uniform over the start circle ∩ arena; roof spots are any prism top ≥ 3 m above the street with room.
7. `have` ignores the weapon in the slot being replaced (swap a laser for a laser is allowed).
8. Weapon timers are frozen while the weapon lies on the ground.
9. Plating heals the HP it adds (like cubes); capacitor raises the energy cap without filling it; `gun_mag` fills on the next reload.
10. If fewer than 3 upgrades are still available, the offer has fewer choices; none → skipped. Offer ids count per mech from 1.

Drops, NPCs, sounds, vision
11. Drops land uniformly within `drops.spread` (0.7) × the radius of the circle the zone is closing to; the ring's vertical reach is one mech height.
12. NPCs close to 20 m and hold; amble at half speed when wandering; aim at the target's center without lead; bullets fly 1.5 × `deaggro_range`; NPC bullets hit mechs only.
13. `laser_charge` sounds once when a charge starts; `jet` every `step_every` while jetting; `crate` when a crate breaks.
14. Trees: crown = ellipsoid (crownR, (height − crownBase)/2) at (crownX, crownY), heights relative to the tree's `z`; transmittance `exp(−canopyDensity × metres of crown)` blocks below `canopyBlockT` (≈ 3.96 m of crown); trunk = cylinder up to crownBase. Inside `near_range` trees are ignored. On the real map this blocks **47%** of 50–200 m sight lines that buildings leave open (3,000 random pairs).
15. Cubes and pickups are seen at their position or 0.6 m above.
16. Zone `center`/`radius` in obs are the phase's start circle (phase 1, unchanged).

## Tests

`python -m unittest discover sim/tests` → **Ran 50 tests, OK** (10 phase 1 + 40 phase 2, ~5 s).

`sim/tests/schema_check.py` is a stdlib draft-07 subset validator over `mechfight.schema.json` (`$ref`, type, const, enum, required, properties, additionalProperties, items, min/maxItems, anyOf/oneOf); events are also checked against their own definition, and `rear`/`effect` must be omitted rather than falsy. It accepts the 300 recorded observations (self-check), rejects one with `zone` removed, and every observation in the phase 2 unit tests plus 60 s of a 16-mech real-map match with fighting/looting bots validates.

## Speed

- Engine only, real map, 16 mechs alive, crude fighting bots, first 90 s of 3 seeds (2,700 decisions): **mean 4.28 ms, p50 4.17, p99 7.62, max 13.2 ms per decision** (observe all + 2 ticks). Target ≤ ~10 ms.
- In the smoke run below (8 parallel matches, 128 bot processes): engine 2.99–4.70 ms/decision per match, mean 3.65; wall 13–18.5 s per match; 16 matches in **38.9 s** total. Late answers 0.0%, max bot latency 21 ms.
- Canopy test: 0.013 ms per sight line.

Readings for the moderator:
- **NPCs are a real cause of death here (3 of our 12 deaths, 37 of the example's 228) and nobody shoots back** (0 NPC kills): both bots ignore `kind: "npc"`. The NPC model carries several assumptions (#12), so treat the size of this effect as uncertain, but "an aggroed drone chases to 90 m and keeps shooting" is the rule as written.
- No bot contests supply drops or breaks crates, so cubes come only from kills (bot 1.19 per match). The economy features are now measurable.
- Matches end at 326–410 s with one mech left, so `hp_at_415s` has no samples in this field.
- 16 matches is small: 4/16 wins has a wide interval.
