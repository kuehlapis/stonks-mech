## Interpretation

This switch did not improve the calibrated proxy. It was about flat on score,
slightly lower on kills, worse on early deaths, and within paired-score noise.
Laser hits received were 58 for `b_peek` vs 67 in the paired rerun baseline,
a reduction of 9 hits, but rear damage and outside-zone rates did not improve
enough to establish a benefit. Keep `PEEK_AFTER_BEAM` OFF; do not stack it
with another switch.
